## Lessons for the protocol

- **Forbid `rm -rf` in worker prompts.** Two reviewers hung on a permission prompt for
  cleaning their own scratch directory. A silent worker after 30 minutes is a stalled one.
- **Record vendor reachability before routing.** grok's absence changed the panel's shape; the
  ledger should carry "reachable this run: yes/no" per vendor.
- **A reviewer's severity is a claim.** Round two moved 9 of 15 business findings and the
  coordinator moved 8 of 31 code findings — mostly down, twice up (the scanner shapes). The
  report is only as credible as that pass.
